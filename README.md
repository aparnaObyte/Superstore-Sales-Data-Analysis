# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis - Power BI Project

This is a 3-page Power BI dashboard built to analyze retail sales data. The goal of this project is to look past high-level sales numbers, find where the business loses money, and identify the root causes.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Structure

* **Page 1: Executive Summary** 📈
  * Shows a high-level overview of store performance. 
  * Total sales reached **$2.30M** with a profit of **$286.40K**. 
  * Flags an overall average discount rate of **15.62%** for further review.

* **Page 2: Regional Performance** 🗺️
  * Breaks down the sales data by geography. 
  * Coastal states like California and New York generate strong profits. 
  * Highlights a major profit problem in the Central region, specifically within **Texas** and **Illinois**.

* **Page 3: Product Profitability** 📦
  * Deep dive into product sub-categories to find out why certain areas lose money.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights Found

* 🛑 **The Biggest Problem:** **Tables** are the largest financial drain for the store, losing over **$17.7K**.
* 💸 **The Root Cause:** Tables lose money because they have a very high average discount of **26.13%**. 
* ⭐ **The Top Performers:** **Copiers** and **Phones** are highly profitable, bringing in over **$100K** together because their discounts are kept low.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* 📋 **Limit Furniture Discounts:** Establish strict caps on discounts for furniture lines—especially tables and bookcases—to protect profit margins.
* 📍 **Review Central Region Stores:** Investigate the specific store strategies in Texas and Illinois to fix the losses happening there.

---
